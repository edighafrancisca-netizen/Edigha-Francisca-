Privacy Notice

What this site collects.

A plain notice explaining what information is gathered when you use this website, why it is gathered, who processes it, how long it is kept, and how to have it removed. Written to be read, not to be scrolled past.

Last updated 12 November 2026
Applies to edighafrancisca.com
Contact for privacy edighafrancisca@gmail.com

In short: this website collects only what is needed to reply to you and to send you the emails you have asked to receive. There is no tracking pixel, no advertising cookie, no behavioural profiling, and no selling of any data to any third party. When you submit a form, the details you provide go to a form processing service and then to a private inbox. Nothing else happens to them.

01What is collected

When you submit the Request a Proposal form or the newsletter form, you provide:

  • Your name and email address. Required to reply to you.
  • Your author or pen name. Optional, for the proposal form.
  • Your book title and Amazon link. Optional in some cases, required in others, and used to study the listing.
  • Your book genre and publishing status. Used to understand the project and the market it is entering.
  • Your goals, challenges and project details. Free-text, written by you, used to consider the enquiry.
  • The services you are interested in. Optional checkboxes, used to understand what you are looking for.
  • Any additional information you choose to provide. Purely optional.

The site does not collect anything else. There is no analytics service, no session recording, no advertising network, and no social media pixel on any page of this site.

02How the information is used

The information submitted through the proposal form is used for one purpose: to reply to you about your book. It is read personally, and it is not shared with any third party for any reason other than the form processing described below.

The information submitted through the newsletter form is used for one purpose: to send you the emails you have asked to receive. You can unsubscribe at any time, and the unsubscribe link is in every email the newsletter sends.

No information is used for profiling, advertising, retargeting, or building any audience other than the newsletter list itself.

03Who processes it

Two services are involved in handling the information you submit.

Formspree receives the contents of the proposal form and the newsletter form when you click the submit button. It forwards them to a private inbox. Formspree processes submissions under its own privacy policy, which is available at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

Google Fonts is used to serve the typefaces this site uses. Google Fonts records the IP address of every device that loads a page. It does not receive any other information about you, and it does not receive anything you submit through any form. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Nothing else

There is no other processor. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which serves files but does not receive or store any form submissions. No email marketing platform beyond Formspree's forwarding is currently involved.

04Cookies and tracking

This site does not set any cookies of its own. There are no analytics cookies, no advertising cookies, and no social media cookies.

Google Fonts, as described above, may record the IP address of every device that loads a page. It does not set cookies for this purpose. If you prefer that Google does not see your IP address, your browser can be configured to block third-party fonts, and the page will still render with a fallback typeface.

There is no way to opt out of anything else, because there is nothing else to opt out of. The only tracking of any kind is the IP address that any web server sees when a page is requested.

05How long it is kept

Proposal form submissions are kept for as long as they are useful to the enquiry. If an engagement proceeds, they are kept for the duration of the engagement and for a reasonable period afterwards. If an engagement does not proceed, they are deleted within twelve months.

Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe. When you unsubscribe, your email address is removed from the newsletter list. Any additional information you supplied at the time of subscribing is removed at the same time.

If you would like your information deleted sooner than either of these periods, email edighafrancisca@gmail.com and it will be deleted within seven working days.

06Your rights

You have the right to see what information is held about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, to object to its use, or to withdraw consent at any time. For the proposal form and the newsletter, these rights can be exercised by emailing edighafrancisca@gmail.com. You do not need to give a reason, and there is no charge.

If you believe your information has been mishandled, you have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country of residence. In Nigeria, that is the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. In the United Kingdom, that is the Information Commissioner's Office. In the European Union, that is the supervisory authority in your member state.

07Children

This site is not directed at children under the age of sixteen. No information is knowingly collected from anyone under sixteen. If you believe a child has submitted information, email edighafrancisca@gmail.com and it will be deleted.

08Changes to this notice

If the way this site collects or handles information changes, this notice will be updated and the date at the top of the page will change. If the change is substantial, it will be noted prominently at the top of the page for at least thirty days after the change.

The notice is not changed to accommodate new services silently. If a new processor is added, it is named in section three, and the date at the top of the page reflects when the addition was made.

09How to contact

Any question about this notice, or any request relating to your information, can be sent to edighafrancisca@gmail.com. The reply will come from the same address. There is no separate privacy officer and no separate form to fill in for a privacy request. The same inbox handles both.

A note on why this notice exists.

Most privacy notices are written to protect the company. This one is written to tell you the truth about what the site does and does not collect. If any part of it is unclear, or if you believe it is missing something, that is worth reporting to the same inbox that handles everything else.
