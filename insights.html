Why publishing a book is only the beginning of its discovery journey

The day a book is published, a small ceremony happens. A file uploads. A page appears. A link is shared. And then the quiet begins. Most authors are unprepared for this quiet, because the work they have spent years on is finished, and the work of being found has not yet started. The gap between those two things is where most books disappear. This essay is about that gap, and about the conditions a reader passes through before a book is chosen. It is not a guide to promotion. It is a map of the ground a book must cross.
