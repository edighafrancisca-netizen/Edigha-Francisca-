Frequently asked questions

About the practice

What this consultancy is, what it is not, and how it sits in relation to the wider book marketing industry.

01 What does Edigha Francisca Literary Solutions do?

It is an independent literary consultancy working with authors on book marketing, Amazon discoverability and author growth strategy. The work is research led: before any recommendation is made, the book, its genre, its readers and its current discoverability are studied. The result is a written set of choices, shaped to one book, that an author can understand, defend and act on.

It is not a digital marketing agency, and it is not a publishing house. It is a small practice built for the writer who has finished the long work of writing and now faces the stranger work of being found.

02 Who can work with the consultancy?

Authors across all publishing backgrounds. Independent authors, self published authors, traditionally published authors, and authors published through hybrid presses. What matters is not how the book was published, but whether the author is willing to look at the book as a stranger would, and to act on what the research finds.

03 Do you work with authors across different genres?

Yes. The method is the same across genres, because it begins with research. What changes between genres is the specific market being studied: the keywords its readers use, the categories they browse, and the books the book will be compared with. A psychological thriller and a cozy mystery ask different research questions, but they are answered the same way.

04 Do you work with self published authors?

Yes. Self published authors are often the ones who benefit most from this work, because they carry the full weight of the marketing decisions themselves. What a self published author often lacks is not effort but orientation: which choices matter, in what order, and why. That is the work.

05 What makes this practice different from other book marketers?

The practice begins with research, and it shows its reasoning. Most book marketing offers a service, a package, or a campaign. This practice offers a study of the book, and the choices that follow from the study. The reasoning behind every recommendation is shared, so that the author understands what is being proposed and why, and can act on it long after the engagement ends.

06 How many clients does the practice take on at once?

A small number, deliberately. Each project involves studying the book, its market, its readers and its competition in detail, and that work does not scale by adding volume. The scope of a typical engagement is measured in weeks, not months, and the practice is limited to the number of books it can read carefully.

Understanding the services

What Amazon keyword research, Author Central and A plus Content actually are, and how the four practices connect to one another.

07 What is Amazon keyword research?

The work of finding out which phrases readers actually type when they are looking for a book like yours, which of those phrases your book can realistically claim, and which are already owned by books that cannot be displaced. It is a study of language and of competition, and it produces the phrases that go into your book's metadata.

08 What is Amazon SEO optimization?

The alignment of a book's title, subtitle, description and backend metadata with the way Amazon search decides who sees the book. It is not an exact science, because Amazon does not publish its algorithm. But the visible evidence, the books that rank for specific phrases, gives clear guidance about what works.

09 What is Amazon Author Central?

The free service that authors use to claim their author page on Amazon. The author page shows a photograph, a biography, and every title the author has published. It is the page most readers reach after finishing one book and deciding whether to read another, and it is the most often neglected page on a book's listing.

10 What is Amazon A plus Content?

The enhanced product page that gives a book visual and editorial space beneath the description. A plus modules can extend what the description said with image and text, or they can repeat what the description already said, which wastes the space. Most books that use A plus Content fall into the second category.

11 How does the research process work?

The book is read as a stranger would read it. Its genre, its current presentation, its keywords, its categories and its competition are studied. The findings are gathered into a working document, and the document becomes the basis for every recommendation that follows.

12 Do you offer customized strategies?

Every strategy is customized, because every strategy is built around one book. No two books have the same readers, the same competition, or the same objectives. The method is the same across projects. The strategy is not.

Working with the practice

How to begin, what the practice does not promise, and what to expect from the first conversation.

13 How can an author request a proposal?

Through the contact page, using the Request a Proposal form. The form asks for the author name, the book title, its current Amazon listing (if live), the genre, the publishing status, and the objectives. A first response is usually within a few working days, and it is a considered reply, not an automated confirmation.

14 Do you publish your prices?

No. Scope and cost are decided after the book has been studied, because a book that needs only two of the four practices should not be charged for all four. An author who has been offered a package before being asked about their book has been sold a product, not a strategy.

15 Can I submit my Amazon book link?

Yes, and it is the most useful thing you can include. The listing is where the research begins. If the book is not yet published, that is fine as well. The work can begin before publication, and in fact often produces more useful results that way.

16 Do you guarantee book sales or rankings?

No one can guarantee sales, rankings or reviews. What can be promised is a clearer view of the book, a better built page, and a positioning chosen for the reader it was meant to reach. Authors should be wary of any consultancy that claims otherwise, and should treat the phrase guaranteed bestseller with the suspicion it deserves.

17 What happens in the first conversation?

The first conversation is a conversation, not a pitch. It begins with the book: what it is, who it was written for, and what the author wants it to do. It ends with a clear sense of whether the practice is the right one for the book, and if so, what the first stage of research would examine. There is no obligation to proceed, and no package is presented before the book has been discussed.

18 What if the practice is not the right fit for my book?

Then the reply will say so. Some books need a different kind of help. Some are not ready for marketing yet. Some need an editor, not a strategist. When the practice is not the right answer, saying so is more useful than taking the work anyway. A short note explaining why, and what might help instead, is part of the reply.
