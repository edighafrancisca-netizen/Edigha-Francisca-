01The Consultancy

A book is not discovered because it exists. It is discovered because someone made it findable.

Edigha Francisca Literary Solutions is an independent literary consultancy working with authors who want to understand the space their book occupies, and the readers it is most likely to matter to. Our work sits at the meeting point of market research, editorial judgement and practical book marketing.

We begin with questions, not templates. How is this book currently discovered? What language do its readers use when searching for it? How does it appear beside the books it will be compared with? Those answers shape every recommendation that follows, from Amazon discoverability to author platform presentation.

A three stage engagement

The order is not a formality. Research that has not been done cannot inform a strategy, and a strategy that has not been chosen cannot support a promotion.

01

Research

We examine the book, the author, the publishing situation, the genre, the target readers, the competitive environment and the book's current discoverability.

02

Strategy

Research is translated into recommendations: positioning, keyword opportunities, presentation improvements and the discovery pathways most relevant to the book.

03

Implementation

Agreed strategies are put into action and refined against the project's objectives, with clear notes on what was done, and why.

03Our Philosophy

Research before recommendations. Strategy before promotion.

We do not begin with a package. We begin with your book, your genre, your publishing situation and the readers you are trying to reach, then we build a strategy that fits that reality.

Evidence over assumption

Recommendations are grounded in what the market shows, not in what is assumed to work.

Clarity over volume

A precise position is more useful than a long list of unconnected marketing activities.

Fit over formula

Each project is assessed on its own terms: its genre, objectives and marketing needs.

The thinking behind the work

Discoverability is not guessed at. It is read from the shelf a book actually sits on, beside the books it will be compared with.
