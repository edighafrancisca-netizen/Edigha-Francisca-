The conversation begins with the book: what it is, who it was written for, and what you want it to do. Fill in the form below and you will receive a considered reply, not an automated confirmation. 01Contact information

A direct line to a real reader.

Every message is read personally. There are no account managers and no automated replies. If the form is not the right shape for what you need to say, the email address below reaches the same inbox.

Email

edighafrancisca@gmail.com

What to include

The book title, the genre, the current Amazon listing if the book is live, and what you want the book to do. Two or three sentences about the book itself help enormously. A proposal is easier to write well when the reply has something to work with.

Response time

Most enquiries receive a reply within three to five working days. If you are working against a launch date, mention it in the message and the reply will move faster.
